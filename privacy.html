Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

What Vault does

Vault is a personal savings tracker. You can enter balances, savings goals, dates, deposits, withdrawals, notes, and reminder preferences.

Where your information is stored

In this version, entered financial records and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Vault does not require an account and the app's code does not send these records to a Vault-operated server. The website is delivered through GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard technical request information under its own policies.

Backups and exports

When you export a backup or CSV, your browser creates a downloadable file containing the selected information. Anyone with access to that file may be able to read it. You control where you save or share exports. JSON backups are not encrypted.

Privacy and security limitations

The optional PIN screen is not encryption or strong authentication. A person with access to the device, browser profile, developer tools, or exported files may be able to access records. The balance-hiding control only conceals values on screen. Do not enter passwords, bank account numbers, Social Security numbers, military operational details, or other highly sensitive information.

Data retention and deletion

Records remain in the browser until you delete them, clear browser/site data, use a different browser profile, or your browser removes stored data. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage may permanently erase records. Vault cannot restore data without a backup you have kept.

Third parties and future changes

This release does not integrate bank connections, advertising trackers, or analytics services. Your browser, operating system, device, and GitHub Pages may handle technical data according to their own policies. Any future online sync or third-party integrations would require an updated privacy notice.

Questions

No dedicated privacy contact is configured in this release. Before inviting the public to use Vault, the operator should publish a working support and privacy contact method.

These notices describe the currently distributed, browser-based version of Vault. If Vault later adds accounts, analytics, cloud syncing, advertisements, or payments, these notices must be reviewed and updated.
